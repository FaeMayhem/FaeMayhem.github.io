About

I'm a content creator and digital producer based in Boston with a background that spans entertainment storytelling, academic media production, and institutional communications. Currently, I manage live historical ghost tours while creating narrative scripts that translate research into engaging experiences for diverse audiences. My work consistently focuses on making complex subject matter—whether historical events, academic theory, or technical processes—accessible and compelling across written, visual, audio, and video formats. I bring both technical production skills and genuine curiosity about how to connect specialized knowledge with the people who need it.
